Student Data and AI Use Policy — teaching example

Purpose
This internal teaching policy describes how student information may be used in AI and analytics projects.

Rules
1. Student data may only be used for a documented educational or operational purpose.
2. Sensitive, financial, location, and biometric attributes require explicit governance approval before use in an AI system.
3. Biometric information must not be used for marketing, personalization, or model training unrelated to identity verification.
4. Student records used for experimentation should be de-identified or synthetic whenever the task does not require identifiable information.
5. Teams must document the source, owner, intended use, and retention period of data used by an AI application.
6. A model may not infer a new sensitive attribute merely because another permitted attribute is available.
7. If a proposed use is not explicitly covered by policy, the Data Steward must escalate the question rather than invent a permission.

This file is synthetic course material and is not an official Tecnológico de Monterrey policy.
