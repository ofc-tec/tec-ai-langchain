Student Information Retention Standard — teaching example

Retention must follow the approved purpose and the minimum period required by the relevant business process.

Default examples:
- academic records: 7 years after last enrollment
- financial aid records: 7 years
- institutional email account data: 2 years after graduation
- derived campus-location observations: 90 days
- biometric templates used for temporary identity verification: delete within 30 days after the approved purpose ends

When two sources specify different retention periods, the Data Steward should identify the conflict and request authoritative review rather than silently selecting one.

This file is synthetic course material and is not an official Tecnológico de Monterrey policy.
